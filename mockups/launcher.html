apptray — tabs as modes

Three fixed tabs: Search is the flat app list you open on, Categories is the groups you can drill into, Power is the session actions. The tab strip never grows, so your tags can multiply without breaking it. Tab or ←→ switches.

click to focus

Keys

a–zFilters the current tab. In Search that's every app; in Categories it filters group names, or the apps inside the group you opened.
Tab ←→Change tab. Your typing carries across — type fire in Categories, Tab to Search, it's still there and now searches everything.
↑ ↓Move the selection. Focus stays in the text box always.
EnterCategory: open it. Power action: run it. App: nothing yet — launching is step 7.
EscClears the query, then leaves the open category, then returns to Search, then quits.
